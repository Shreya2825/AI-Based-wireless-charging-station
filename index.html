🧠 Why Batteries Explode & How AI Prevents It

Thermal Runaway occurs when a lithium-ion cell's internal temperature rises beyond control:

  1. High current → heat buildup inside cell
  2. Electrolyte decomposes → produces flammable gas
  3. Gas expands → mechanical stress → cell rupture
  4. Chain reaction → neighbouring cells ignite → explosion

AI Prevention:

  • Real-time temperature & voltage monitoring (ML model)
  • Anomaly detection using Random Forest classifier
  • Thermal risk score regression (0–100)
  • Auto cut-off if risk > 75 or temp > 65°C
  • Emergency alert broadcast to user & operator
